Use your own timetable

  1. Sign in to the new Student LfY and click My studies in the top menu.
  2. Open the TEACHING TIMETABLE tab.
  3. Find the line “It is currently pre-enrolment period for the upcoming academic year.” (the wording changes during the year; it's the sentence just above the Autumn Term table).
  4. With your mouse, click and drag to select everything below that line: start at Autumn Term and go down to the last row of the last table. Ctrl+A doesn't work on this page.
  5. Copy it (Ctrl+C, or ⌘C on a Mac), then paste it into the box below (Ctrl+V / ⌘V). Your week appears straight away.

It's fine if your selection includes extra words or headings. Only the timetable rows are read.

Your timetable is read inside this page and kept only in this browser. Nothing is uploaded, and the page never asks for your LSE login.

Add to your calendar

Creates one event per session, for the right weeks only. Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and Outlook can all import the .ics file. Check the Week 1 Mondays against LSE's published term dates first.

Downloads are blocked in this preview. Copy the text, save it in a plain text file named timetable.ics, then import that file into your calendar.

The .ics file won't open if you double-click it. Import it into your calendar instead:

Google Calendar
On a computer, open calendar.google.com. Click the gear icon, then Settings → Import & export. Choose the file, pick a calendar and click Import.
Apple Calendar (Mac)
Choose File → Import, pick the file, then choose a calendar.
Outlook
In Calendar, click Add calendar → Upload from file, pick the file, then choose a calendar.

Tip: Create a new calendar called "LSE timetable" first and import into that. If something changes later, you can delete that calendar and import a fresh file without touching your other events.
